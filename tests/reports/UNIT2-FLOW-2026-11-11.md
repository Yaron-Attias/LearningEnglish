## Accepted behavior

- The four exercise tiles each open or automatically resume their own saved session, including current prompt, option order, submitted answer, feedback and completed results.
- There is no generic Continue control and no start confirmation dialog. Switching exercises preserves the other sessions.
- Quiz results remain available until the learner explicitly starts a new round from that exercise's results screen.
- Photos remember the last visited word, allow free previous/next navigation and show meaning and audio controls throughout. The final word remains a photo card, without a terminal results screen.
- Opening or refreshing boots to the home of the highest numbered published unit that contains content. The relevant tile then resumes the saved exercise.

## Persistence and compatibility

The existing localStorage key remains wordsteps-grade8-unit2-v1. Storage schema 2 holds independent sessions under mode:0 through mode:3, focus:1 through focus:3, and word:<id>. A legacy single session migrates into the corresponding slot; word statistics are retained rather than reset. Unit 1 uses its existing separate data. Content-version invalidation may discard incompatible sessions while retaining cumulative word statistics.

The earlier 11 November confirmation guard is historical and superseded by independent exercise sessions. No start confirmation is expected in the current design. All 20 worksheet frames are retained unchanged. Other learning modes, Present Simple and penalty gameplay retain their behavior.
